# Sharkbay.shop - Official Enterprise Knowledge Base & Customer Support Manual

## 1. Company Overview & Core Value Proposition
- **About Us**: sharkbay.shop is Malta's premier localized e-commerce platform, designed to be the "Amazon of Malta". We are built locally for the local community, offering an extensive product catalog, hyper-fast delivery, and competitive pricing.
- **Local Infrastructure**: Unlike overseas platforms facing customs delays and high shipping fees, our dedicated local warehouse is based right here in Malta, ensuring immediate inventory processing, local pickups, and seamless island-wide distribution.

- **Support Channels**:
